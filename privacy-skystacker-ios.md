# Sky Stacker for iOS Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 29, 2026

This policy describes the Sky Stacker game for iPhone.

## Information handled on your device

Sky Stacker has no account system and no developer-operated server. The game saves only your best scores (including today's Daily Challenge best) and your sound, vibration, and tutorial settings. These are stored locally on your iPhone and are never sent to LWS.

## What Sky Stacker does not collect

Sky Stacker does not collect personal information, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers. It does not request any iOS permissions. It works fully offline.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Sky Stacker does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

## Purchases

Sky Stacker is free and has no in-app purchases.

## Support requests

If you choose to contact support, LWS receives the email address and message you send and uses them only to respond to your request.

## Your choices

You can reset your saved scores and settings at any time by deleting the app. You can ask a privacy question at [contact.lws.co@gmail.com](mailto:contact.lws.co@gmail.com).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
