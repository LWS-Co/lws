## How to play

- **Tap** to drop the sliding block on your tower. Anything hanging over the edge is sliced off.
- **Hold** your finger down to slow time, then let go to drop. The slow-motion meter refills with perfect drops and when you reach a new zone.
- **Perfect drops** score double. Three in a row make your block wider.
- **Special blocks:** Gold (triple points on a perfect drop), Ice (keeps sliding after release), Rubber (widens the tower on a good landing), Ghost (blinks in and out).
- **Gems and shields:** collect gems with perfect drops. Five gems earn a shield that saves you from one total miss.
- **Wind:** above 15 blocks, gusts push the block. Watch for the warning banner.
- **Daily Challenge:** the same block sequence for everyone each day.

## Common questions

**I can't hear any sound.** Sky Stacker follows the iPhone silent switch. Turn off silent mode, and check that sound is on in the game's main menu.

**How do I reset my best score?** Scores are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them.

**Does the game need internet?** No. Sky Stacker works fully offline.
